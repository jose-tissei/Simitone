Sims remaster · UI kit v1 · 01 Foundations
Plastic, glossy, a little silly.
Simitone's teal, white pills and dark docks stay. What changes: structure, sizes, state and spacing. Two themes share every token name: Night (Simitone dark, default) and Sunny Lot (classic daylight).
Sul sul!
1 · Say it twice
Never colour alone. Pair hue with an icon, label or pattern. Fixes the red-only Room bar and the red memory warnings.
2 · 48 and 16
Hit targets ≥ 48 px, essential text ≥ 16 px at 1080p. All sizes multiply by UIScale (100 / 125 / 150%).
3 · Group, then label
No more flat rows of 12 icons. Sections get headings, toggles show On/Off, destructive actions sit apart.
4 · Contained dialogs
Dialogs are centred cards (max 1100 wide), not edge-to-edge bars. One primary action, bottom right.
5 · World vs. chrome
Balloons, pie menu and tooltips float over the world: dark glass, theme-independent. Docks and panels follow the theme.
6 · Quirk in motion
Gloss, plumbob, bounce easing and Simlish microcopy carry the Sims feel. Never in the layout grid.

Plastic lift: hard 5px offset shadow in a darker tone of the fill, then a soft 10px blur. Buttons press down 3px and squash to 96%.
Easing: bounce cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) for pops and hovers (180 ms), ease-out (.2,.8,.2,1) for panels sliding (320 ms). Plumbob bobs every 2.4 s. Respect reduce-motion.
Elevation: three levels. Flat (cards), lift (panels, dialogs), float (balloons, pie, tooltips: dark glass 90%).
