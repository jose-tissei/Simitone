How to build a new screen or menu
Give this page to your AI agent along with design-tokens.json and UI-GUIDE.md. Every new screen starts from the recipe, passes the checklist, and ships in both themes.

Which container for what
UseForRules
Dock tabAlways-near info about the active SimMax 7 tabs, labelled, badge for unread.
Modal panelBrowse, read, manage (Memory, Options)Head band, one scroll area, Esc closes.
DialogA decision or a one-off message≤ 2 buttons, primary bottom right, ≤ 60 words.
SheetQuick choice tied to a Sim (Talk)Centred card, keys 1–9 pick, never full width.
ToastSomething happened, optional actionTop centre, 6 s, hover pauses, max 2 stacked.
Pie menuVerbs on a Sim or object≤ 8 slots, icon + label, submenu marked “…”.
BalloonSpeech and thoughts in the worldDark glass, theme independent, ≥ 20 px text.
TooltipExact values and “why disabled”Appears after 300 ms, never holds the only copy of a fact.
The 12-step recipe
1
Name the jobOne sentence: “The player opens this to ___.” If you need “and”, make two screens.
2
Pick the containerUse the table. Prefer dock tab or toast over a new modal.
3
Pick the sizeModal S 640, M 960, L 1360. Height follows content to a max of 800, then scroll.
4
Lay out on 8 pxPadding 28–36, gaps from the spacing scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48). Left list 340, content flex.
5
Head band firstIcon, title (Fredoka 28), optional subtitle chip, close. Close is always top right and Esc.
6
One primary actionMint pill, bottom right. Secondary is white pill, destructive is coral and confirms.
7
Components onlyCompose from the kit (button, tab, chip, row, need, meter). A new component needs all its states drawn.
8
Say it twiceEvery status has colour + icon or label or pattern.
9
Write in Sims voiceShort, warm, a bit silly. Verb on buttons (“Say it”, “Invite”), never “OK” unless informing.
10
Add the quirkOne touch only: gloss, plumbob, bounce, or a Simlish empty state.
11
Draw the statesDefault, hover, pressed, focus, disabled, empty, loading, error, long text, 150% scale.
12
Ship both themesReview in Night and Sunny Lot, then export layout JSON and skins.
Checklist before merge
✓ Targets ≥ 48 × 48, 8 px apart.
✓ Text essential ≥ 16 px, body 18, titles Fredoka. Nothing truncates mid-word.
✓ Contrast 4.5:1 text, 3:1 icons and focus ring, in both themes.
✓ Colour-blind safe no meaning by red/green alone.
✓ Input mouse, keyboard (Tab order, Enter, Esc) and focus ring on everything.
✓ Scale still fits at 125% and 150%; ultrawide centres, 16:10 keeps safe margins.
✓ Motion every animation has a reduce-motion path.
✓ Mod items flagged with the “Mod” chip in Options so players know what is native.
Voice and Simlish
Do: “Betty wants to chat about night street food.” “Nothing here yet. Go meet a neighbor!”
Don't: “Error 0x41.” “Operation completed successfully.”
Empty states: a sleeping plumbob and one friendly line, plus the one action that fixes it.
Placeholders: italic Simlish is allowed in demos (“Sul sul!”) but never as the only label.
